About Best4U Offers

Best4U Offers is a personal automation tool used to publish promotional content from the Telegram channel @best4uoffers to the owner's Pinterest account.

The application processes promotional images, descriptions, and product links and creates corresponding Pins on the authorized Pinterest account.

How It Works

The automation receives promotional posts from the owner's Telegram channel, processes the available content, and publishes Pins to the owner's Pinterest boards using the Pinterest API.
