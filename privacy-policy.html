Privacy Policy

Best4U Offers

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Best4U Offers handles information when using the Best4U Offers personal automation tool.

Best4U Offers is a personal automation tool used by its owner to process promotional content from the Telegram channel @best4uoffers and publish corresponding Pins to the owner's authorized Pinterest account.

1. Information We Access

The application may access promotional content published on the owner's Telegram channel, including:

  • Images attached to Telegram posts.
  • Text and descriptions contained in posts.
  • Product and promotional links included in posts.

The application may also access information from the owner's Pinterest account that is necessary to operate the Pinterest API, including authorized boards and Pins.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the application is used only to operate the personal automation workflow.

The application may use this information to:

  • Process promotional content from the Telegram channel.
  • Transfer promotional images, descriptions, and links into Pinterest Pins.
  • Create Pins on the owner's authorized Pinterest boards.
  • Prevent duplicate processing of previously processed Telegram posts.

3. Pinterest API

Best4U Offers uses the Pinterest API to interact with the owner's authorized Pinterest account.

Pinterest information is accessed only with authorization from the account owner and is used only for the functions described in this Privacy Policy.

The application does not access Pinterest accounts, private boards, Pins, or personal information belonging to other Pinterest users.

4. Telegram

The application processes content from the owner's Telegram channel:

https://t.me/best4uoffers

Telegram content is processed only for the purpose of creating and publishing promotional Pins through the authorized Pinterest account.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information processed by the application is not provided to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data may be transmitted to Pinterest only when necessary to perform the requested Pinterest API operations.

6. Data Storage

The application is designed to minimize the storage of personal information.

Authentication credentials and API tokens are treated as confidential information and are used only to authenticate the authorized services.

The application does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials and information processed by the application.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the automation and provide its intended functionality.

The application does not maintain a database of personal information belonging to Pinterest users.

9. User Control

The Pinterest account owner can revoke the application's authorization at any time through the Pinterest account settings.

The automation can also be stopped by its owner at any time.

10. Third-Party Services

The application interacts with third-party services, including:

  • Telegram
  • Pinterest
  • GitHub Pages for hosting this website

These services have their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or its functionality.

Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated date.

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Best4U Offers automation tool, please contact the owner through the Telegram channel:
